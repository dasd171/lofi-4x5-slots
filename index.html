Paytable

Each row counts matching symbols from the left (the YETI WILD substitutes for any regular symbol). Longer runs pay more; multipliers apply to the total bet. Payout tiers: 3 / 4 / 5 of a kind.

Special Rules

  • YETI WILD: substitutes for every symbol except the tape; a full row of 5 yetis pays 10,000x and takes the Aurora Jackpot.
  • TAPE Scatter: 3 or more anywhere on the grid trigger free spins (3 → 8 spins, 4 → 12, 5 → 20, 6+ → 30) and pay instantly by count.
  • Aurora Jackpot: 2% of every paid spin feeds the pool; any full row of 5 of a kind takes it.
